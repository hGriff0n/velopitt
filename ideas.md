### Short Term
#### Segments
- Highlight popular or interesting segments in various regions of the metro
	- Aiming for a mix of newbie and experienced riders
- Highlight common trails and bike routes
	- Highlight areas to avoid if possible
- Clicking on a segment will zoom in to the specific segment and show some side windows
	- Detailed look at gradient profile (for climbs)
		- Maybe also color the graphed line
	- Related climbs and variations
		- Also extensions/continuations, where do people often go from this climb
	- Pacing notes, general road conditions, interesting spots
	- General pacing calculator
- https://github.com/visgl/deck.gl?tab=readme-ov-file for more advanced vis?
	- Segments should be generated from json/markdown files
- At low zooms, an overlay should be enabled that displays the "zonal" breakdown

##### What is a "Related Climb"
- For example: 18th Street
- Variations are `St.Patrick`, `Opporto`, `Josephine`
- Extension would be `18th+Arlington`

#### Routes
